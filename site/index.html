The Classroom Reality

The problem, in all its ugly glory

Students learn a concept on Monday, pass the quiz on Friday — and 30 days later, up to 80% of it is gone. Not because teachers didn’t teach it, or students didn’t try. That is simply how human memory decays without timed retrieval.

01

Teach, test, evaporate

Students cram on Thursday night, pass on Friday, and blank out a month later — right when the next unit builds on those exact foundations.

02

The re-teaching treadmill

Weeks of precious class time vanish into re-explaining material that was already “covered” in September. Pacing slips; advanced topics get cut.

03

Why proven tools haven’t reached K–12

Cognitive science solved retention decades ago with Spaced Repetition (SRS). But solo adult tools like raw Anki weren’t built for 30 eighth-graders, a school syllabus, or a teacher’s 45-minute prep period.

04

Commercial EdTech makes it worse

Per-seat subscriptions, student email sign-ups, ad networks, and learning histories piped to third-party clouds — a legal and ethical minefield for school administrators.

Schools haven’t rejected the science of memory. They simply lacked classroom infrastructure that respects teacher time, cohort logistics, and student privacy. AnkiTov was built to be that missing layer.

Effortless Adoption

How AnkiTov fixes it — in minutes, not months

Designed alongside classroom reality: zero extra lessons to plan, zero individual accounts to troubleshoot, and zero change to how you teach during the period.

Step 1 • ~2 minutes

Drop in a deck & share a 6-letter class code

Import any standard Anki .apkg deck — or start immediately with our open curriculum seed decks. Assign to your class in two clicks. No student emails, passwords, or IT tickets required.

Step 2 • minutes a day

At home, on the bus, or as a 4-minute bell-ringer

Students review at home or during the 4-minute warm-up while you take attendance. The FSRS scheduler surfaces only the cards about to fade — weaving September’s foundations together with this week’s unit.

Step 3 • Glance before class

Spot class-wide gaps before Friday’s quiz

Your teacher console turns individual review logs into a live retention view. If half the class is slipping on a specific formula or vocabulary set, you see it immediately — and spend two minutes addressing it, not two weeks re-teaching.

For IT Directors & School Leaders

What it takes to run a school pilot

Any hardware you already own

Runs on a single school Mac, Windows PC, or Linux box on your local LAN — or a $5/month private VPS. Easily serves 200+ concurrent students over school Wi-Fi.

Single binary, zero dependencies

One self-contained executable with embedded SQLite. No external database server, no Docker required, no outbound internet access needed.

Zero-PII audit posture

Students join via an anonymous 6-letter class code. No student names, emails, or device identifiers ever leave your building.
