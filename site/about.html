Our Mission

Evidence-based retention for every student.

Students spend countless hours preparing for exams only to forget the material weeks later. We exist to change that through well-engineered, privacy-respecting spaced repetition tools.

The Core Thesis

The spacing effect and active recall are among the most robust findings in cognitive psychology. Yet traditional classrooms often rely on massed study right before tests.

AnkiTov was formed to bring spaced repetition systems (SRS) into structured education in a way that complements teachers rather than disrupting classroom rhythm.

Guiding Principles

  • Spaced repetition works: We focus on proven retrieval practice mechanisms without exaggerated claims.
  • Classroom privacy first: Protecting middle-school and high-school students is non-negotiable. Zero third-party telemetry, zero ad models.
  • Teacher autonomy: Software should assist educators with scheduling and review tracking, leaving pedagogical control in teacher hands.
  • Open formats: Knowledge cards and learning history belong to the students and schools, freely exportable and portable.

Who We Are

AnkiTov is built as an independent, solopreneur-led educational initiative. We value lean, robust engineering, clear documentation, and direct collaboration with classroom educators.
